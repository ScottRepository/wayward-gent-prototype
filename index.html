HOW TO USE WAYWARD GENT

1. Cocktail Infusions (Jars & Sachets)

Whether you purchased our 12oz glass mason jar or our eco-friendly $16 refill sachet, making craft cocktails is simple:

  • Fill: Pour 12oz (a standard 375ml mickey) of your favourite spirit into the jar. Gin, vodka, bourbon, rum, and tequila all work wonderfully.
  • Steep: Seal the jar tightly and leave it at room temperature for 72+ hours. Give it a gentle swirl once a day.
  • Serve: Once infused to your desired richness, strain and pour 1.5–2 oz over ice. Top with club soda, tonic, ginger beer, or lemonade.
  • Pro-Tip: Don't throw away the ingredients! Infuse a second time for 5–7 days, or steep in hot water for a fragrant, spiced mocktail.

2. Freeze-Dried Simple Syrup Pucks

We invented our freeze-dried syrup pucks to eliminate "expiry date panic". Store the dry puck in your pantry for months with zero stress:

  • Simmer: In a small saucepan, combine 1 puck with 1 cup of water over medium heat until dissolved. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  • Store: Once cool, bottle and refrigerate. Use ½ to 1 teaspoon per cocktail or iced latte.
  • Freeze: Won't use it in 2–3 weeks? Pour into small ice cube trays and freeze!

3. Flavoured Cocktail Rimmers

Moisten the rim of your glass with a fresh lime wedge or a dab of simple syrup, then gently roll the outer lip through the rimmer tin. Each tin generously coats 12–15 cocktails.

OUR STORY

Welcome to Wayward Gent Cocktails! We are a small, passionate Canadian business founded in Norwood, Ontario, with a big dream: making high-brow, lounge-quality cocktails effortless for the home bartender.

Wayward Gent was born inside our beloved local bakery, The CakeHouse. As bakers and flavour enthusiasts, we spent years obsessing over spices, aromas, and balance. Whenever friends gathered at our home, we loved serving upscale cocktails—but hated spending $150 on specialty liqueurs and throwing out half-used bottles of expired simple syrup the following week.

We knew there had to be a better way. We began dehydrating real, locally sourced fruits, hand-blending botanicals and bitters, and packing them into cocktail infusion kits and shelf-stable simple syrup pucks.

Today, every single jar, refill sachet, rimmer, and syrup puck is hand-packed with care right here in Norwood. Whether you're mixing a Caesar at the cottage in the Kawarthas, hosting a holiday party, or shipping a gift box to friends across Canada, we hope our blends bring ease, celebration, and delicious sips to your glass.

Cheers,
Tam & The Wayward Gent Team
